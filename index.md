# Privacy Policy — Firm Foundation

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

Firm Foundation is an iOS app that reads your heart rate from the Apple Health
app and sends you a Bible verse when your heart rate rises. This policy
explains what the app collects, what it does with it, and what it does not do.

The short version: your health data stays on your iPhone. It is never uploaded
to us or to anyone else.

## Health data

With your permission, Firm Foundation reads the following from the Apple
Health app:

- **Heart rate** — to detect when your heart rate rises above your usual range
- **Resting heart rate** — to work out what "usual" means for you, rather than
  using a fixed number
- **Active energy burned** — to recognise when a raised heart rate is likely
  exercise, so the app can stay out of the way
- **Sleep analysis** — to work out when you are usually asleep, so the app can
  stay quiet during those hours

**How this data is used.** All of it is read and processed on your device. The
app compares your current heart rate against your own baseline to decide
whether to send you a verse. Nothing more.

**What we do not do with it.** Your health data is never transmitted off your
device. We never see it. Specifically, health data is:

- **Never** sold, rented, or traded
- **Never** used for advertising or marketing, by us or anyone else
- **Never** shared with data brokers, analytics providers, or third parties
- **Never** used for any purpose other than deciding when to show you a verse

Firm Foundation only writes to your Health app if you explicitly ask it to; it
does not add data to Health in normal use.

You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.
The app will stop reading your data immediately. Verse detection will stop
working, since it has nothing to work from.

## Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your iPhone and is not uploaded anywhere:

- Your settings (sensitivity, quiet hours, exercise suppression, background
  choice)
- Your history of delivered verses
- Verses you have saved
- Which verses seem to help you most, used to choose better verses over time
- Any background photo you add from your photo library

All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

---

## Information we receive

**Verse text.** The app downloads its library of Bible verses from Google
Firebase, which we use to host them. This is a one-way download: the app asks
for the verses and receives them. No health data, no settings, and nothing
identifying you is included in that request, and the app never writes anything
back.

As with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to Google as
the hosting provider, and Google's software assigns your installation of the
app an anonymous identifier. Neither is connected to your health data, and we
do not use either to identify or track you.

**No accounts.** Firm Foundation has no sign-in and no user accounts. We hold
no profile of you and nothing that could identify you.

**No analytics.** The app does not use analytics or tracking software. We do
not collect usage data, and we do not know which features you use or how often
you open the app.

## Subscriptions and payment

Firm Foundation is a paid app sold by subscription through the App Store.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, card
number, or billing address. We receive only anonymous, aggregated sales
information from Apple, which cannot be linked back to you.

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account. To cancel, open the
Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

---

## Notifications

Verse notifications are created on your device by the app itself. They are not
push notifications sent from a server, and their content never leaves your
phone.

## Children

Firm Foundation is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13.

---

## Your choices

- **Stop health access** — Health app → Sharing → Apps → Firm Foundation
- **Stop notifications** — iOS Settings → Firm Foundation → Notifications
- **Clear your verse history** — in the app, on the History tab
- **Delete everything** — delete the app from your iPhone

Because your data is stored on your device rather than on our servers, there is
no account for us to delete and no copy of your information for us to hand
over. Deleting the app removes it.

---

## Not medical advice

Firm Foundation is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or
monitor any condition. A raised heart rate has many ordinary causes. If
anything about your health concerns you, please speak to a doctor.
